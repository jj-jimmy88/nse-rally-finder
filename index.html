NSE Small-Cap Rally Finder

Runs automatically every trading day after the NSE close (GitHub Actions). Goal: find NSE stocks about to gain +50% within 40 trading days, using only what was knowable on the day. Universe each day = stocks passing your hygiene screen on that day (P/E < 50, market cap > ₹1,000 cr, annual profit growth > 20%). Buy = next day's open. Learned on 2020–22, tested on 2023–26. Every trade the two rules ever produced is in the chart and table below.

Rule 1 · R-A Breakout at the top of a big run

  1. Passes the hygiene screen today (P/E < 50, mcap > ₹1,000 cr, profit growth > 20%)
  2. Close ≥ 97% of its 52-week high (at or within 3% of the high)
  3. Close ≥ 3× its 52-week low (already tripled)
  4. Market cap < ₹2,000 cr
  5. Today's open was < 2% above yesterday's close (no gap-up)
  6. Month is April–December

Rule 2 · PB Promoter buying into momentum

  1. Passes the hygiene screen today
  2. A promoter bought shares in the open market in the last 90 days (NSE insider filings)
  3. Close ≥ 90% of its 52-week high
  4. Up > 10% in the last 20 trading days and > 20% in the last 60
  5. Market cap < ₹3,000 cr
Execution: buy at the next day's open, −10% stop loss on the intraday low, hold 40 trading days. One signal per stock per 56 days. Take every signal — it is a portfolio rule, not a stock pick.

Backtest — every closed trade, each in exactly one band (peak within 40 days)
